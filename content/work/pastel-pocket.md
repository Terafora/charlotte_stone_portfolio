## A space that belongs to the young person

Pastel Pocket explores what a care-related app can feel like when it begins with the young person’s agency rather than the organisation’s records. The prototype brings journalling, voice messages, appointments, documents, rewards and shared objectives into one personal dashboard.

## The design direction

The interface uses soft colour, generous spacing and tangible controls without becoming childish. Multiple palettes, larger text and reduced-motion settings are available from the same prototype.

The dashboard language stays direct and reassuring: **your space, your way.** Actions describe what will happen, and a person can save progress on a shared form instead of being pushed through it in one sitting.

## What the prototype tests

- Whether private reflection and communication can live comfortably beside formal objectives.
- How status and progress can be visible without feeling judgemental.
- How a strong visual personality can still support accessibility preferences.
- Where local prototype data is enough to test an experience before backend work begins.

## What I learned

A friendly layer is not a substitute for agency. The most important interaction decisions were about pacing, reversibility, clarity and giving someone a meaningful way back.
